### Taxonomic Hierarchy
- The taxonomic categories from species to kingdom are arranged in **ascending order** (Figure 1.1).
- Taxonomists have also developed sub-categories within this hierarchy for more sound and scientific placement of taxa.
- As we go higher from species to kingdom, the number of **common characteristics goes on decreasing**.
- **Lower the taxa, more are the characteristics that the members within the taxon share.**
- **Higher the category, greater is the difficulty of determining the relationship to other taxa at the same level.**
- This makes the problem of classification more complex.

$$
\text{Species} \uparrow \text{Genus} \uparrow \text{Family} \uparrow \text{Order} \uparrow \text{Class} \uparrow \text{Phylum/Division} \uparrow \text{Kingdom}
$$
