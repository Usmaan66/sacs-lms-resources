### 1.2.3 Family
- The next category is **Family**, which groups related genera with a still lesser number of similarities compared to genus and species.
- Families are characterized based on both **vegetative** and **reproductive features** of plant species.
- Examples:
    - Among plants, three different genera (*Solanum*, *Petunia*, and *Datura*) are placed in the family **Solanaceae**.
    - Among animals, genus *Panthera* (lion, tiger, leopard) is placed along with genus *Felis* (cats) in the family **Felidae**.
    - Cats and dogs, despite some similarities, are separated into two different families: **Felidae** and **Canidae**, respectively.
