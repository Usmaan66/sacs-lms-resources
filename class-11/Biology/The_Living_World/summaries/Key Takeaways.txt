### Key Takeaways:
- **Taxonomic categories** form a hierarchy, from species (lowest) to kingdom (highest).
- Each category (taxon) represents a unit of classification.
- Moving up the hierarchy, the number of shared common characteristics decreases.
- **Species**: group of individuals with fundamental similarities and distinct morphological differences.
- **Genus**: group of related species with more common characters than species of other genera.
- **Family**: group of related genera with fewer similarities than genus and species.
- **Order**: assemblage of families with a few similar characters.
- **Class**: includes related orders.
- **Phylum/Division**: comprises related classes (Phylum for animals, Division for plants).
- **Kingdom**: highest category, grouping various phyla/divisions.
