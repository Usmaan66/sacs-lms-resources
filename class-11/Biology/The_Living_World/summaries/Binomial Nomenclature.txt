### Binomial Nomenclature
- Introduced by *Carolus Linnaeus*.
- Each name has *two parts*:
  - *Genus*: First word (capitalized)
  - *Specific epithet*: Second word (lowercase)
- Written in *italics* (or underlined when handwritten), e.g., Mangifera indica.
- Author’s name may follow, e.g., Mangifera indica Linn.
