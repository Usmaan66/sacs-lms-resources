### 1.2.7 Kingdom

- All animals belonging to various phyla are assigned to the highest category called **Kingdom Animalia** in the classification system of animals.
- The **Kingdom Plantae**, on the other hand, is distinct, and comprises all plants from various divisions.
- Henceforth, we will refer to these two groups as animal and plant kingdoms.
- The taxonomic categories from species to kingdom have been shown in ascending order starting with species in Figure 1.1.
- These are broad categories. However, taxonomists have also developed sub-categories in this hierarchy to facilitate more sound and scientific placement of various taxa.
- As we go higher from species to kingdom, the number of common characteristics goes on decreasing.
- Lower the taxa, more are the characteristics that the members within the taxon share. Higher the category, greater is the difficulty of determining the relationship to other taxa at the same level. Hence, the problem of classification becomes more complex.
