### 1.2.4 Order
- Categories like species, genus, and families are based on a number of similar characters.
- **Order** and other higher taxonomic categories are generally identified based on the **aggregates of characters**.
- Order is a higher category that is an assemblage of families exhibiting a few similar characters.
- The similar characters are fewer in number compared to different genera included in a family.
- Examples:
    - Plant families like Convolvulaceae and Solanaceae are included in the order **Polymoniales**, mainly based on floral characters.
    - The animal order **Carnivora** includes families like Felidae and Canidae.
