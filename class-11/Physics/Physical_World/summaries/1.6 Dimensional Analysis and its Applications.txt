### 1.6 Dimensional Analysis and its Applications
- **Purpose of Dimensional Analysis:** This powerful technique uses the concept of dimensions to:
    1.  Check the consistency (correctness) of equations.
    2.  Deduce relationships between different physical quantities.
- **Basic Principle:**
    - Only physical quantities with the **same dimensions** can be added or subtracted. (You can't add velocity to force!)
    - In multiplication or division, units (and dimensions) are treated like algebraic symbols and can be cancelled.
    - Both sides of a mathematical equation must have the **same dimensions**.
