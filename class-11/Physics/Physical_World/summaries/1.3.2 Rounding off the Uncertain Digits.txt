### 1.3.2 Rounding off the Uncertain Digits
- When a calculation yields more digits than are significant, the result needs to be **rounded off**.
- **Rules for Rounding Off:**
    1.  **If the digit to be dropped is greater than 5:** The preceding digit is increased by 1.
        - *Example:* 2.746 rounded to three significant figures becomes **2.75**.
    2.  **If the digit to be dropped is less than 5:** The preceding digit is left unchanged.
        - *Example:* 1.743 rounded to three significant figures becomes **1.74**.
    3.  **If the digit to be dropped is exactly 5:**
        * **If the preceding digit is even:** The digit is simply dropped (preceding digit remains unchanged).
            - *Example:* 2.745 rounded to three significant figures becomes **2.74**.
        * **If the preceding digit is odd:** The preceding digit is raised by 1.
            - *Example:* 2.735 rounded to three significant figures becomes **2.74**.
- **Intermediate Calculations:** In multi-step calculations, it's good practice to keep **one more digit** than the significant figures needed in the final answer during intermediate steps. This prevents rounding errors from accumulating. Round off to the correct number of significant figures only at the very end of the calculation.
- **Exact Numbers in Formulas:** Exact numbers like '2' or '$\pi$' in formulas (e.g., $T=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$) have an infinite number of significant figures and do not limit the significant figures of the result. For $\pi$, you can use 3.142 or 3.14 depending on the required precision.

**Example 1.1: Surface Area and Volume of a Cube**
- Side length = 7.203 m (4 significant figures)
- **Surface Area:** $6 \times (7.203~m)^2 = 311.299254~m^2$
    - Since the original length has 4 significant figures, the area must also be rounded to 4 significant figures.
    - Area = $\textbf{311.3~m^2}$
- **Volume:** $(7.203~m)^3 = 373.714754~m^3$
    - Similarly, the volume must be rounded to 4 significant figures.
    - Volume = $\textbf{373.7~m^3}$

**Example 1.2: Density Calculation**
- Mass = 5.74 g (3 significant figures)
- Volume = 1.2 $cm^3$ (2 significant figures)
- **Density:** $Mass / Volume = 5.74~g / 1.2~cm^3 = 4.7833...~g/cm^3$
    - The least number of significant figures is 2 (from volume). So the density should be expressed to 2 significant figures.
    - Density = $\textbf{4.8~g/cm^3}$
