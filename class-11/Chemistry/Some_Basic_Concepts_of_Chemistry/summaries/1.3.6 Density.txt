### 1.3.6 Density

-   **Density**: The amount of mass per unit volume of a substance.
-   **Formula**:
    $$Density = \frac{Mass}{Volume}$$
-   **SI unit of density**: Derived from SI units of mass (kg) and volume ($m^3$).
    $$SI~unit~of~density = \frac{kg}{m^3}~or~kg~m^{-3}$$
-   **Common laboratory unit**: $g~cm^{-3}$ (grams per cubic centimeter) due to smaller masses and volumes.
-   **Concept**: A higher density means the particles of a substance are more closely packed.
