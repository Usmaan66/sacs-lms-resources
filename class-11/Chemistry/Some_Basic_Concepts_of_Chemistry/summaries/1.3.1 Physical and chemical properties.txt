### 1.3.1 Physical and chemical properties

-   Every substance has unique properties. These are categorized as:
    -   **Physical Properties**: Can be measured or observed without changing the substance's identity or composition.
        -   Examples: **Color**, **odor**, **melting point**, **boiling point**, **density**.
    -   **Chemical Properties**: Require a chemical change to occur during their measurement or observation.
        -   Examples: **Composition**, **combustibility**, **reactivity with acids and bases**, **acidity**, **basicity**.
-   Chemists use knowledge of these properties to describe, interpret, and predict substance behavior, determined through careful measurement and experimentation.
