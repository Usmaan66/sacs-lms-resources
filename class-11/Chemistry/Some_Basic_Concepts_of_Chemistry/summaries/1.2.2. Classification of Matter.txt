### 1.2.2. Classification of Matter

-   At the macroscopic (bulk) level, matter can be classified into **mixtures** or **pure substances**.
-   These can be further subdivided:
    -   **Pure Substances**
        -   **Elements**
        -   **Compounds**
    -   **Mixtures**
        -   **Homogeneous Mixtures**
        -   **Heterogeneous Mixtures**

**Key Takeaways**:
-   **Mixtures** have variable composition, **pure substances** have fixed composition.
-   **Elements** are the simple pure substances.
-   **Compounds** are formed from elements combining chemically in fixed ratios.
