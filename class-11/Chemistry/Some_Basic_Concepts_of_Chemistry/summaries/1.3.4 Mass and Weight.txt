### 1.3.4 Mass and Weight

-   **Mass**: The amount of matter present in a substance. It is a constant value.
-   **Weight**: The force exerted by gravity on an object. It can vary depending on gravity (e.g., different on Earth vs. Moon).
-   Mass can be accurately determined using an **analytical balance** in a laboratory.
-   **SI unit of mass**: **Kilogram** (kg).
-   **Common laboratory unit**: **Gram** (g), since smaller amounts of chemicals are used.
    -   Relationship: $1~kg = 1000~g$
