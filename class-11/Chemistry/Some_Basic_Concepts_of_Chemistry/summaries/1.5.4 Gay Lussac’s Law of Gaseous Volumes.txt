### 1.5.4 Gay Lussac’s Law of Gaseous Volumes

-   Given by **Gay Lussac** in 1808.
-   **Statement**: When gases combine or are produced in a chemical reaction, they do so in a simple ratio by volume, provided all gases are at the same temperature and and pressure.
-   **Example**: 100 mL of hydrogen combine with 50 mL of oxygen to yield 100 mL of water vapour.
    -   Hydrogen + Oxygen $\rightarrow$ Water
    -   100 mL + 50 mL $\rightarrow$ 100 mL
    -   The volumes of hydrogen and oxygen (100 mL and 50 mL) combine in a simple ratio of $2:1$.
-   This law is considered the law of definite proportions by volume.
