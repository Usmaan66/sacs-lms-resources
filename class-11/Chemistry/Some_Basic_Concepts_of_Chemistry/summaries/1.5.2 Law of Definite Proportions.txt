### 1.5.2 Law of Definite Proportions

-   Given by French chemist **Joseph Proust**.
-   **Statement**: A given compound always contains exactly the same proportion of elements by weight (or mass), irrespective of its source or method of preparation.
-   **Example**: Proust studied cupric carbonate, finding the same percentage composition of copper, carbon, and oxygen in both natural and synthetic samples.
    -   Natural Sample: Cu (51.35%), C (9.74%), O (38.91%)
    -   Synthetic Sample: Cu (51.35%), C (9.74%), O (38.91%)
-   Also known as the **Law of Definite Composition**.
