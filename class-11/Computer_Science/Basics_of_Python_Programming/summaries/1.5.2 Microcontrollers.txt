#### 1.5.2 Microcontrollers

- A **microcontroller** is a tiny, self-contained computer on a **single integrated circuit (chip)**.
- Unlike a microprocessor (which is just the CPU on a chip), a microcontroller includes the **CPU, a small, fixed amount of RAM, ROM, and various other input/output peripherals all together on one chip**.
- **Characteristics**:
    - Designed specifically for **embedded systems** and **dedicated tasks**. They are not general-purpose computers.
    - Much **smaller in size** and **lower in cost** than microprocessors or full computer systems.
    - They are embedded within other devices to control a specific function or task.
- **Real-life Examples**:
    - The chip inside your **keyboard** or **mouse**.
    - The control unit in a **washing machine** that manages cycles.
    - The brain of a **digital camera**.
    - The tiny computer in a **pen drive**.
    - Inside **remote controllers** for TVs.
    - The timer and control unit in a **microwave oven**.
- **Benefit**: Microcontrollers allow for the automation of repetitive and precise tasks within devices, making our daily lives easier by reducing the need for human intervention.
