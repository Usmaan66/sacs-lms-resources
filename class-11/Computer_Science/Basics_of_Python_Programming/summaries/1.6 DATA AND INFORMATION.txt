### 1.6 DATA AND INFORMATION

- The most fundamental function of a computer is **processing data**.
- Anything that a computer handles—whether it's instructions, pictures, songs, videos, or documents—is considered **data**.
- **Data**: Refers to raw, unorganized facts, figures, or symbols that do not have meaning on their own until processed.
    - **Example**: Just numbers "10, 20, 30" are data.
- It's crucial to understand the difference between **data, information, and knowledge**, as these terms are often confused.
