#### Reproductive Phase

- The period when an organism is capable of sexual reproduction.
- Marked by flowering in higher plants.
- In animals, it involves changes in hormone levels, leading to reproductive cycles.
- Examples of reproductive cycles in mammals:
    - **Oestrous cycle**: Observed in non-primate mammals (e.g., cows, sheep, rats, deers, dogs, tiger) where distinct changes occur in ovaries and accessory ducts, and the females are receptive to mating only during the oestrus period.
    - **Menstrual cycle**: Observed in primates (e.g., monkeys, apes, humans) where there is a periodic bleeding (menstruation) and the females are receptive to mating throughout the cycle.
- Seasonal breeders: Organisms that reproduce only during specific favorable seasons.
- Continuous breeders: Organisms that can reproduce throughout their reproductive phase.
