### Life Span

- The period from birth to the natural death of an organism is known as its **life span**.
- Life spans of organisms vary greatly, from a few days to several thousand years.
- Example: Mayfly (1 day), Butterfly (1-2 weeks), Rice plant (3-4 months), Rose (5-7 years), Crow (15 years), Dog (10-13 years), Crocodile (60 years), Human (70-100 years), Parrot (140 years), Banyan tree (200-500 years), Tortoise (100-150 years).
- The life span of an organism is not necessarily correlated with its size. For instance, crows and parrots are similar in size, but their life spans are very different. A mango tree has a much shorter life span compared to a banyan tree.
- Death is a certainty for every single organism, i.e., no individual is immortal, except single-celled organisms.
- Single-celled organisms (e.g., *Amoeba*, bacteria) are considered immortal because they divide into two daughter cells, effectively continuing their lineage without an organismal death.
