#### Zygote

- The diploid cell formed by the fusion of male and female gametes.
- It is the vital link that ensures the continuity of species between one generation and the next.
- The development of the zygote depends on the type of life cycle of the organism and the environment it is exposed to.
- In organisms with a haplontic life cycle (e.g., algae, fungi), the zygote undergoes meiosis to form haploid spores that germinate into haploid individuals.
- In organisms with a diplontic or haplodiplontic life cycle, the zygote undergoes mitosis.
