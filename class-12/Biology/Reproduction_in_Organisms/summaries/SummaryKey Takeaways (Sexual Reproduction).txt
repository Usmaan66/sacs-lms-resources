### Summary/Key Takeaways (Sexual Reproduction)

- Sexual reproduction involves gamete formation and fusion, leading to genetically varied offspring.
- Life cycles include juvenile/vegetative, reproductive, and senescent phases.
- Pre-fertilisation events include gametogenesis (formation of haploid gametes) and gamete transfer.
- Fertilisation (syngamy) is the fusion of gametes, forming a diploid zygote, and can be external or internal.
- Post-fertilisation events include zygote development and embryogenesis, leading to the formation of a new individual.
