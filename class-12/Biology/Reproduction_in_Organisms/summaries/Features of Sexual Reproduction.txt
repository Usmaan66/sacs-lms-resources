### Features of Sexual Reproduction

- **Sexual reproduction** involves the formation and fusion of gametes, leading to the formation of a zygote.
- It is a complex and slower process compared to asexual reproduction.
- Involves two parents (usually), but can sometimes involve a single parent (e.g., bisexual organisms).
- Offspring produced are not identical to the parents or to each other, leading to **variation**.
- Variation is crucial for evolution.
