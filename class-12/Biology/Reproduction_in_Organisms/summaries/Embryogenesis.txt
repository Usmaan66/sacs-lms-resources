#### Embryogenesis

- The process of development of the embryo from the zygote.
- Involves two major processes:
    - **Cell division (mitosis)**: Increases the number of cells in the developing embryo.
    - **Cell differentiation**: Cells undergo modifications to form specialized tissues and organs.

##### Development of Zygote (Animals)

- **Oviparous animals**: Lay eggs. The development of the zygote takes place outside the female parent's body.
    - Example: Reptiles, birds.
    - Eggs are usually covered by a hard calcareous shell for protection.
    - Incubation period required for embryo development.
- **Viviparous animals**: Give birth to live young. The development of the zygote takes place inside the female parent's body.
    - Example: Most mammals, including humans.
    - Greater chances of survival of the young due to proper embryonic care and protection inside the mother's body.

##### Development of Zygote (Flowering Plants)

- Zygote forms inside the ovule.
- After fertilisation, the zygote develops into the **embryo**.
- The ovules develop into **seeds**.
- The ovary develops into the **fruit**.
- The fruit develops a protective wall called the **pericarp**.
- Petals, sepals, and stamens often wither and fall off.
- The fertilized ovule contains the embryo. The seed germinates under favorable conditions to produce a new plant.
