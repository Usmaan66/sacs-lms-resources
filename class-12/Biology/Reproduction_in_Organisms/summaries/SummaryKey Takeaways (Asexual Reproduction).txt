### Summary/Key Takeaways (Asexual Reproduction)

- Asexual reproduction involves a single parent and produces genetically identical offspring (clones).
- Various modes exist, including binary fission (Amoeba, Paramecium), budding (Yeast, Hydra), spore formation (zoospores in Chlamydomonas, conidia in Penicillium, gemmules in sponges), and vegetative propagation in plants.
- Vegetative propagules are specialized structures for asexual reproduction in plants.
