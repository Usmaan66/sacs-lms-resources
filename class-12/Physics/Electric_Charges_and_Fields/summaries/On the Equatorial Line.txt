#### On the Equatorial Line

-   The **equatorial line** is the line perpendicular to the axial line and passing through the center of the dipole.
-   The electric field $E$ at a point on the equatorial line at a distance $r$ from the center of the dipole is:
    $$E_{\text{equatorial}} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{p}{(r^2 + a^2)^{3/2}}$$
-   For a **short dipole** ($r \gg a$):
    $$E_{\text{equatorial}} \approx \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{p}{r^3}$$
-   **Direction:** Opposite to the direction of the dipole moment.
