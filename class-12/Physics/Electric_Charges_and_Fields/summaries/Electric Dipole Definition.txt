### Electric Dipole Definition

-   An **electric dipole** consists of two equal and opposite point charges ($+q$ and $-q$) separated by a small distance $2a$.
-   The **dipole moment ($\vec{p}$)** is a vector quantity that describes the strength and orientation of an electric dipole.
-   Magnitude of dipole moment: $p = q \times 2a$
-   Direction of dipole moment: From the negative charge ($-q$) to the positive charge ($+q$).
-   The SI unit of dipole moment is **coulomb meter (C m)**.
