## Key Takeaways

-   **Electric charge** is fundamental, conserved, and quantized.
-   **Coulomb's Law** describes the force between point charges.
-   An **electric field** is a force field created by charges, affecting other charges.
-   **Electric field lines** are a visual representation of the field's direction and strength.
-   **Electric flux** measures the number of field lines passing through a surface.
-   **Gauss's Law** simplifies the calculation of electric fields for symmetric charge distributions.
-   An **electric dipole** is a pair of equal and opposite charges, characterized by its dipole moment.
-   Understanding **continuous charge distributions** allows for calculating fields from extended objects.
