#### Quantisation of Charge

-   The charge on any object is always an **integral multiple** of a basic unit of charge, $e$.
-   This basic unit of charge is the magnitude of charge on an electron or a proton, $e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$.
-   The formula for quantisation of charge is: $q = ne$
    -   Where $q$ is the total charge, $n$ is an integer ($1, 2, 3, \dots$), and $e$ is the elementary charge.
-   **Discovery:** This principle was experimentally demonstrated by **Millikan's oil drop experiment** (1909).
