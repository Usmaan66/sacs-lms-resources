#### Electric Field due to a Uniformly Charged Infinite Plane Sheet

-   Consider a cylindrical or cuboidal Gaussian surface piercing the sheet, with its flat ends parallel to the sheet.
-   The electric field is perpendicular to the sheet.
-   By Gauss's Law, the electric field $E$ near a uniformly charged infinite plane sheet with surface charge density $\sigma$ is:
    $$E = \frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}$$
    -   Where $\sigma = Q/A$ is the surface charge density (charge per unit area).
-   **Note:** The field is independent of the distance from the sheet for points close to the sheet.
