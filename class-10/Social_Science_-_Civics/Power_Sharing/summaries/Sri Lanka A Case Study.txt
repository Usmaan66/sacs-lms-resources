### Sri Lanka: A Case Study

- Sri Lanka is an island nation with a diverse population.
- **Ethnic composition**:
    - **Sinhala-speakers**: 74% of the population, mostly Buddhists.
    - **Tamil-speakers**: 18% of the population, includes 'Sri Lankan Tamils' (13%) and 'Indian Tamils'.
    - **Christians**: 7% of the population, who are both Tamil and Sinhala.
- After gaining independence in 1948, the Sinhala community, being the majority, sought to establish its dominance.
- **Majoritarianism**: The belief that the majority community should be able to rule a country by disregarding the wishes and needs of the minority.
- Sri Lanka's democratically elected government adopted majoritarian measures to assert Sinhala supremacy.
