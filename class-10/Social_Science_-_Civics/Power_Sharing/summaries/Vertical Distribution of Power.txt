### Vertical Distribution of Power

- Power is shared among governments at **different levels**.
- This includes a general government for the entire country (Central or Union government) and governments at the provincial or regional level (State governments).
- The constitution clearly lays down the powers of different levels of government, as was done in Belgium but not in Sri Lanka.
- This is also known as a **federal division of power**.
- This principle can be extended to lower levels of government like municipalities and panchayats.
