### Power Shared among Political Parties, Pressure Groups, and Movements

- In a democracy, citizens can choose among various contenders for power through competition between political parties.
- This competition ensures that power does not remain in one hand for long.
- When two or more parties form an alliance to contest elections and win, they form a **coalition government** and share power directly.
- **Pressure groups** and movements representing different interests (e.g., traders, farmers) also share power by influencing government decisions.
