### Belgium: A Case Study

- Belgium is a small European country with a complex ethnic composition.
- **Ethnic composition**:
    - **Flemish region**: 59% of the population, speaks Dutch.
    - **Wallonia region**: 40% of the population, speaks French.
    - **German-speaking**: 1% of the population.
    - **Capital city (Brussels)**: 80% French-speaking, 20% Dutch-speaking.
- The French-speaking minority was wealthy and powerful, which was resented by the Dutch-speaking community who got the benefits of economic development and education much later.
- This led to tensions between the two communities, especially in Brussels, where the Dutch were a minority despite being a majority in the country.
