### Horizontal Distribution of Power

- Power is shared among different **organs of government** at the same level.
- These organs are the legislature, executive, and judiciary.
- This system ensures that no single organ has unlimited power, as each checks the others.
- This arrangement is called a **system of checks and balances**.
- **Example**: In a democracy, ministers are responsible to Parliament or State Assemblies, and judges can check the laws made by the legislature and the functioning of the executive.
