### Calculating Nutritional Status
BMI is used to determine if an adult is undernourished, normal, or overweight.

**Formula for BMI:**
$$\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{Weight (in kg)}}{\text{(Height in meters)}^2}$$

**BMI Standards:**
- **BMI < 18.5:** Person is undernourished.
- **BMI > 25:** Person is overweight (obesity).
