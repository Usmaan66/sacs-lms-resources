### Examples of Varying Developmental Goals
| Category of Person | Developmental Goals / Aspirations |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Landless rural labourers** | More days of work, better wages, and quality education for children. |
| **Prosperous farmers** | High family income through higher support prices and cheap labour. |
| **Urban unemployed youth** | Better job opportunities and stable income. |
| **A girl from a rich family** | As much freedom and opportunity as her brother; sharing of household work. |
| **Adivasis (Tribals)** | Protection of their land and local water sources (e.g., small check dams). |
