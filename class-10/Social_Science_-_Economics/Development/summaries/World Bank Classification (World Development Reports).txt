### World Bank Classification (World Development Reports)
The World Bank uses Per Capita Income (in US$) to classify countries based on 2023 data:
- **High Income (Rich) Countries:** Per capita income of **US$ 63,400** per annum and above.
- **Low Income Countries:** Per capita income of **US$ 2,400** or less.
- **India:** Categorized as a **low middle income country** with a per capita income of **US$ 10,030** per annum.
