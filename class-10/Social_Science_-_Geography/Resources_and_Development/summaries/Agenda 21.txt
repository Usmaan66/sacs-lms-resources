### Agenda 21
- **What it is:** A declaration signed at the United Nations Conference on Environment and Development (**UNCED**).
- **Goal:** To achieve global sustainable development and combat environmental damage, poverty, and disease through global cooperation.

---
> **Summary:** Indiscriminate resource use leads to inequality and environmental ruin. Sustainable development, highlighted by the 1992 Earth Summit and Agenda 21, is the only way to ensure the planet's future.
