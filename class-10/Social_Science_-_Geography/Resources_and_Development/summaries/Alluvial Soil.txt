### Alluvial Soil
- **Distribution:** Entire Northern Plains and river deltas of the East Coast.
- **Components:** Sand, silt, and clay.
- **Classification by Age:**
  - **Bangar:** Old alluvial, contains more 'Kanker' nodules.
  - **Khadar:** New alluvial, more fine particles and more fertile.
- **Crops:** Sugarcane, paddy, wheat, and pulses.
