### Conservation of Resources
- Resources are vital, but irrational consumption leads to socio-economic and environmental problems.
- **Gandhian View:** "There is enough for everybody's need and not for anybody's greed."
- **Important Publications:**
  - *Small is Beautiful* (1974) by Schumacher.
  - *Our Common Future* (Brundtland Commission Report, 1987) introduced 'Sustainable Development'.
