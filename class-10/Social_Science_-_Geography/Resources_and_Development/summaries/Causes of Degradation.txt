### Causes of Degradation
- **Deforestation and Mining:** Major causes in Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, and Odisha.
- **Overgrazing:** Major cause in Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra.
- **Over-irrigation:** Causes water logging, salinity, and alkalinity in Punjab, Haryana, and Western Uttar Pradesh.
- **Industrial Effluents:** Water and land pollution from factory waste.
