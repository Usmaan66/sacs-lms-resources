## Land Use Pattern in India

- The use of land is determined by **physical factors** (topography, climate, soil) and **human factors** (population density, technology, culture).
- **Total Geographical Area:** 3.28 million sq km.
- **The Forest Goal:** The National Forest Policy (1952) outlined that 33% of the area should be under forest to maintain ecological balance. Currently, it is much lower.
