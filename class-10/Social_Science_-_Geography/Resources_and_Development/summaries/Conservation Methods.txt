### Conservation Methods
- **Contour Ploughing:** Ploughing along contour lines to slow down water flow.
- **Terrace Farming:** Cutting steps on slopes (common in Western/Central Himalayas).
- **Strip Cropping:** Growing strips of grass between crops to break the wind's force.
- **Shelter Belts:** Planting rows of trees to stabilize sand dunes.

---
> **Key Takeaway:** Soil is precious and takes ages to form. Different regions of India have specific soil types suited for different crops, and using conservation techniques like terrace farming or shelter belts is essential to prevent the loss of this vital resource.
