## Development of Resources
- Resources are vital for human survival and maintaining the quality of life.Historically, it was believed that resources were free gifts of nature, leading to their indiscriminate use and several major problems:

- **Resource Depletion**: Resources are being exhausted to satisfy the greed of a few individuals[cite: 54].
- **Social Inequality**: Resources have accumulated in only a few hands, dividing society into "haves" and "have-nots" (the rich and the poor).
- **Ecological Crises**: Indiscriminate exploitation has led to global warming, ozone layer depletion, environmental pollution, and land degradation.
